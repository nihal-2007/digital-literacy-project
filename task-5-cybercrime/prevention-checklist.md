### 📝 Prevention Checklist
```
- Keep your mobile updated with the latest security update and
your banking apps.
- Use strong, unique passwords for every account and manage them using
a password manager.
- Back up critical files frequently to an external drive or cloud service.
- Verify identities of callers or messages before taking any action
related payments.
- Do not scan QR codes from unverified sources.
- Install reputable antivirus software and run regular scans.
- Always remember entering UPI Pin or sharing OTP can lead to sending
money not recieving it.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links or opening unexpected attachments, even
from known contacts.
```
### 🔍 Reporting Cybercrime
```
- Report incidents immediately at: https://cybercrime.gov.in
- Call the Cybercrime Helpline: 1930 (available 24x7)
```
