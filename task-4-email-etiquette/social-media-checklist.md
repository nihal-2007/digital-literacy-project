# 🌐 Social Media Do's and Don'ts for College Students 
### ✅ Do's:
```
- Protect your privacy by limiting personal information access to the public
- Think Twice before posting content on the internet
- Use Two-Factor Authentication and unique passwords for different sites
- Use social media for building connections with your peers and learining new, useful skills
- Use these platforms to share positive & creative content
```

###  ❌ Don'ts:
```
- Avoid participating in heated internet conflicts that provide no value to anyone
- Be selective with friend requests to avoid scammers or impersonators
- Don't click suspicious emails or links as they may be scams
- Avoid sharing sensitive information like address, phone number
- Don't believe everything you see on the internet
```
